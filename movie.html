Mickey 17 (2025)

Sci-Fi / Adventure

Mickey 17 is an employee sent to colonize a freezing world. Whenever he dies, a new clone is created with his memories. In this sci-fi thriller directed by Bong Joon-ho, Mickey Barnes faces impossible odds on an ice world, until he accidentally meets another clone of himself who is determined to survive.
